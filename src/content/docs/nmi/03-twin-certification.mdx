> *In this page:* the twin certification program (TW-1): the promise it
> certifies, the probe procedure, and the certificate’s suspension
> semantics.

---

## 1. The promise under certification

The twin certificate certifies one promise: **the twin’s indication
equals the physical instrument’s indication, within the Recommendation’s
envelope, at the time of the probe.** The certificate binds a specific
twin endpoint to a specific instrument instance, for a declared
Recommendation and accuracy class.

This is deliberately not a cryptographic claim. The binding is
declarative: it is a claim about correspondence, tested by procedure,
recorded as evidence. Signing has its place for multi-component devices
(a device assembled from SMART-twin-capable components, where each
component’s certificate composes), but the primitive certificate rests
on the probe.

## 2. The probe procedure (TW-1, D2)

The probe is a procedure, not an inspection of code. It runs through
the declared probe channels, each carrying its declared traceability
limitation:

1. **Reference instrument.** A calibrated reference drives the
   physical instrument (a calibrated weight on the load cell, a
   reference gas on the analyzer). The twin’s served indication is read
   at the same time.
2. **Observer attestation.** A human reads the physical display and
   attests it (the epistemic wall: the observation is admissible
   evidence, with its declared limitation and its ±½ graduation
   uncertainty riding the record).
3. **Simulated ground truth.** In development and training, the
   simulated twin’s own ground truth stands in, plainly marked as
   simulation.

The twin passes the probe when the served indication and the reference
agree within the envelope at every probed point, and the served
freshness holds at every read. The evidence records every probe: the
channel, the reference value, the served value, the serve time, the
delta, the verdict.

## 3. The verdict chain

The twin certificate rides the same chain as the type evaluation: the
probe evidence feeds the verdict engine, the engine judges against the
certified promises, the certificate issues when every probed promise
holds. The certificate itself carries the promises-as-verified
projection, so anyone reading it sees what was probed, when, and how it
judged.

## 4. Suspension semantics

A twin certificate is a living claim, and its lifecycle matches:

- **A drift degrades.** A served indication outside the envelope flags
  the certificate; the flag is recorded with the drift evidence.
- **An outage is indeterminate.** A twin that stops serving, or serves
  stale, does not fail: it degrades to indeterminate, with health events
  logged. A silent pass is the one outcome the chain refuses.
- **A re-judgment re-rides the chain.** Corrected evidence (a repaired
  endpoint, a renewed probe) re-judges the certificate against the same
  promises. Suspension and reinstatement are recorded with provenance,
  never silent edits.
